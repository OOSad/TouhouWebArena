# Hakugyokurou Stairs Stage Assets (Temporary Calibration Placeholders)

## Provenance & Compliance Notice
- **Source**: Sourced from *Touhou 09: Phantasmagoria of Flower View* (`th09.dat` -> `world03.anm` and `world03.std` -> `stg5bg.png`), originally adapted from *Touhou 07: Perfect Cherry Blossom* Stage 5.
- **Status**: **Strictly temporary calibration placeholders** used exclusively for setting up 3D geometry coordinates, camera pitch/FOV, UV mapping ratios, and fog shaders.
- **Compliance**: As specified in `ASSETS_LICENSING.md` and `AGENTS.md`, these textures must be replaced with original or permissively licensed fan art prior to public release.

## Texture Files
- `stair_relief.png`: $254 \times 254\text{ px}$ carved stone bas-relief (peonies, pine needles, swirling water rings, plover birds) for step treads and balustrade faces.
- `stair_riser.png`: $254 \times 32\text{ px}$ dark beveled front edge drop for the 3D step risers.
- `stair_balustrade.png`: $32 \times 254\text{ px}$ stone balustrade curb for left railing (extracted from left edge of `stair_relief.png`: trellis at base, round peony, plum blossoms).
- `stair_balustrade_right.png`: $32 \times 254\text{ px}$ stone balustrade curb for right railing (extracted from right edge of `stair_relief.png`: trellis at top, chrysanthemum, pine needles/maple at base).
- `ground_tile.png`: $254 \times 254\text{ px}$ golden earthen courtyard ground plane visible outside the right balustrade during diagonal view.
- `cloud_a.png`, `cloud_b.png`, `clouds.png`: Alpha-keyed drifting mauve/crimson Netherworld cloud wisps.
